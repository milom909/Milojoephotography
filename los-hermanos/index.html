Why it matters

Larkspur floods. We've watched it happen.

On January 4, 2026, a king tide pushed Corte Madera Creek over its banks and into the Larkspur Marina neighborhood. By noon, about half the streets were underwater. Most homes there sit a couple of feet up. The garages don't, and the garages took the worst of it. In one garage on Riviera Circle, the water reached about 4 feet.

Who we are

Local kids protecting our neighbors

We're students at the public schools right here. This is our town. When the creek comes up, it's our neighbors, our teachers and our friends' families who end up with water in the garage.

So we started Los Hermanos to do something about it. We show up, we do the heavy lifting, and we stack every wall like it's protecting our own house. Every job also teaches us how to run a real business, and we take that seriously.

When you hire us, you're getting your home protected and backing kids from your own community.
